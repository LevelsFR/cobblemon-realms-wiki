## 📊 Detailed riding stats

These three optional tables are **for comparing stats**, not for browsing the entire mount roster. Riding stats are separate from battle stats. Columns show configured ranges for **Acceleration**, **Skill** (handling), **Speed**, **Stamina** and **Jump**.

A Pokémon can appear more than once when **different mods** or **different environments** define different stats. The **Configuration** column combines the provider code and riding style. Add-on ranges can exceed 100.

## 🎮 How to ride a Pokémon

1. Send out a Pokémon with a riding definition.
2. **Crouch (Shift) and right-click** it, then choose the ride interaction.
3. Use your movement keys. Steering depends on the riding style. **Shift** normally dismounts.

**No saddle is required.** A Pokémon may have a land, water or air riding style, sometimes more than one.

## 🧭 Movement styles

| Environment | Styles and behavior |
| --- | --- |
| 🌍 **Land** | **Standard**: horse-like movement and sprinting. |
| 🌊 **Water** | **Boat**: surface travel; **Submarine**: diving; **Dolphin**: diving and leaps. |
| ☁️ **Air** | **Bird**: versatile flight; **Jet**: fast flight without hovering; **Hover**: precise movement; **Rocket**: fast forward hover-style flight. |

**Stats explained:** Speed sets top speed; Acceleration sets responsiveness; Skill improves handling; Stamina powers special actions; Jump governs vertical movement differently for each riding style. Some riding stats can be improved using [Aprijuices](../cobblemon-craft/aprijuice-guide.md).

## ⚙️ Useful settings

Camera and riding options are in `config/cobblemon/main.json`. To reduce camera roll, change the **existing** option to `"disableRoll": true`. Do not replace the entire configuration file.
