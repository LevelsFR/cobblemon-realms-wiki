# 🚀 Start Here: Your First Adventure

{% hint style="info" %}
**Welcome to Cobblemon Realms!** This checklist follows the official **v6.3** experience. Complete each step in your own time. Progress is saved **only in this browser**, not in your Minecraft account or on the server.
{% endhint %}

## 🎮 Choose your adventure

**Full** offers the broader survival and building experience, including Create in v6.3. **Lite** focuses on the Pokémon adventure with fewer heavy side systems. These editions have their own downloads and server packs.
