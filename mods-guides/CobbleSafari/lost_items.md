# ✨ Lost Items & Auspicious Poké Balls

{% hint style="info" %}
<p align="center">Find collectibles in dungeon areas and explore the many new appearance, loot added in CobbleSafari 0.4.</p>
{% endhint %}


## 🎁 What Are Lost Items?

A **Lost Item** is a dungeon collectible. Some use a Poké Ball model; others look like buried objects or sparkling ground. Collectibles can have their own loot and count toward dimensional objectives.

## 🎨 Available Looks

| Look | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Rubble** | Original buried object style |
| **Classic** | Traditional Poké Ball appearance |
| **Buried** | Partially embedded item |
| **Floating** | A Poké Ball floating and rotating above its shadow |
| **Sparkles** | A Legends Z-A-style ground sparkle, picked up by walking into it |

The collectible's Poké Ball can use a variety of Cobblemon ball models.

## 🛡️ How Do I Collect Them?

Interact with dungeon collectibles to claim their rewards. Lost Items, Lost Notes and Auspicious Poké Balls **cannot be broken in survival mode**.

## 🧭 Relevant Locations

- [Hyperspace Lumiose](hyperspace_lumiose.md), including floating and sparkling collectibles;
- [Bell Tower](bell_tower.md), with corrected Lost Item distribution in 0.4.1;
- [Dimensional Objectives](dimensional_objectives.md), where some tasks reward collecting Lost Items.
