# 🌃 Ultra Megalopolis

{% hint style="info" %}
<p align="center">A lightless Ultra Space city introduced as a new dungeon in CobbleSafari 0.4.0. It serves as the entry point to the <strong>Ultra Warp Ride</strong>.</p>
{% endhint %}


## 🗺️ Exploration and Entry

Ultra Megalopolis is part of the dungeon portal system. The entrance was visually updated in **0.4.1** to look like an **Ultra Wormhole** rather than an ordinary Hoopa portal. The Megalopolis city also received a visual overhaul inspired by Ultra Sun and Ultra Moon.

{% hint style="info" %}
The portal is an instance entrance. Like other 0.4 dungeons, its entrance portal can expire while its instance remains active for players still inside.
{% endhint %}

## 🪽 Ultra Warp Ride

Look for the Ultra Warp Ride device during your visit. A flight begins a separate exploration challenge: collect energy, avoid obstacles, travel farther and aim at an available wormhole. Explore [Ultra Warp Ride](ultra_warp_ride.md) before entering.

## 🎯 New Objectives in 0.4.1

The dimension's default objectives are chosen from a weighted list, and **task counts may vary per instance**.

| Objective family | Default target range |
| --- | --- |
| Collect energy boosts during the ride | 4–8 |
| Clear or survive warp obstacles | 4–8 |
| Reach warp distance milestones | 1–4 |
| Capture a Pokémon | 1 |
| Claim Lost Items | 1–3 |

Tasks reward loot from the Megalopolis objective table. The upstream definition **disables a separate final-completion reward**, so do not promise a guaranteed golden-ball reward here.

## ⏳ Time Management

The warp system uses the remaining **parent dungeon session**. The upstream settings include a **180-second minimum** to open the ride device and **30 seconds** kept in reserve; emergency energy drain starts when the available time becomes low. These numbers can be configured by the server.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Cobblemon Realms:** some encounters, rewards and timers may differ from the base CobbleSafari mod. Follow the information shown in game.
{% endhint %}
