# 🏙️ Hyperspace Lumiose | Wild Zones & Objectives

{% hint style="info" %}
<p align="center">Discover an unusual, dreamlike version of Lumiose City in CobbleSafari 0.4. The dungeon combines <strong>themed Wild Zones</strong>, rare encounters, obstacles and objectives.</p>
{% endhint %}


## 🌟 Instance Rarity

Each Hyperspace Lumiose instance is assigned a **1-star to 5-star** rank and **one or two Pokémon types**. These characteristics influence its available wild Pokémon and challenges.

| Feature | What to expect |
| --- | --- |
| 1–5 stars | The instance's rarity level |
| One or two types | Influences encounter themes |
| Wild Zones | Clusters of species within the city |
| Obstacles | Breakable or clearable challenges |
| Shard Clusters | Collectible resource nodes, if enabled |
| Dimensional Objectives | Tasks to finish for dungeon rewards |

## 🎯 Objective Examples

Possible tasks include:

- Claiming floating Lost Items or sparkling collectibles;
- Capturing Pokémon, designated species or types;
- Capturing Alpha or Shiny Pokémon when the task appears;
- Clearing **boulders, brambles and sludge**;
- Catching Pokémon through battle or without alerting them.

Complete all required objectives to make the **Golden Auspicious Poké Ball** appear with special rewards. The actual task set is selected for the instance.

## ⏳ Respawns

In the base mod, obstacles return after around **5 minutes** and Shard Cluster resources after around **15 minutes**. Shard Clusters may not appear in every world.

## 🍩 Make Donuts

Collect dungeon ingredients and visit a **Cooking Pot** to prepare donuts with encounter-related or stat-related bonuses. [See the donut guide](hyperspace_donuts.md).

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Cobblemon Realms:** some encounters, rewards and timers may differ from the base CobbleSafari mod. Follow the information shown in game.
{% endhint %}
