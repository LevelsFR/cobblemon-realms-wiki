# 🌌 Ultra Space | Exploration Guide

{% hint style="info" %}
<p align="center">Travel beyond ordinary dimensions with CobbleSafari 0.4's <strong>Ultra Megalopolis</strong>, <strong>Ultra Warp Ride</strong> and <strong>seven Ultra Dimensions</strong>.</p>
{% endhint %}


## 🧭 Where to Start?

The **Ultra Megalopolis** is a dungeon and staging point for the Ultra Warp Ride. The ride is an Elytra-style flight minigame where you gather energy, dodge hazards and choose a wormhole destination. Completing the journey may lead to an Ultra Dimension with a special Pokémon encounter.

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### 🌃 Explore Ultra Megalopolis
Enter through a supported dungeon portal and find the Ultra Warp Ride device. [Read the Megalopolis guide](ultra_megalopolis.md).
{% endstep %}
{% step %}
### 🪽 Start the Ultra Warp Ride
Steer through the tunnel, collect boosts and avoid obstacles. [Learn the controls and rewards](ultra_warp_ride.md).
{% endstep %}
{% step %}
### 🌀 Choose a wormhole
Portal appearance and distance determine the type and difficulty of your destination.
{% endstep %}
{% step %}
### 🐾 Explore and encounter a Pokémon
Each Ultra Dimension offers an encounter whose IVs, bonus moves and Shiny/Alpha potential can be influenced by ride performance. [Compare all seven destinations](ultra_dimensions.md).
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}
