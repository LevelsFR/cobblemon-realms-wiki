# 🌍 Worlds & Dimensions

Explore the worlds, dimensions and instanced destinations documented for Cobblemon Realms. **Not every destination works the same way:** some are standalone worlds, while others are temporary areas reached through CobbleSafari's portals.

## 🌌 Main Destinations

| Destination | What to expect | Guide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ☁️ **The Aether** | A separate world with its own exploration content | [The Aether](aether.md) |
| 🖤 **Deeper and Darker** | A different realm with unique terrain and challenges | [Deeper and Darker](deeper_and_darker.md) |
| 🏝️ **Safari Dimension** | Timed Pokémon-catching activities in type-themed biomes | [Safari Dimension](../CobbleSafari/safari_dimension.md) |
| 🌌 **Ultra Space** | Ultra Megalopolis, Ultra Warp Ride and the documented Ultra Dimensions | [Ultra Space](../CobbleSafari/ultra_space.md) |

## 🧭 Ultra Space Travel

The [Ultra Megalopolis](../CobbleSafari/ultra_megalopolis.md) is the documented starting point for [Ultra Warp Ride](../CobbleSafari/ultra_warp_ride.md), which can lead to [seven Ultra Dimensions](../CobbleSafari/ultra_dimensions.md). These areas should not be confused with permanently accessible Overworld biomes.
