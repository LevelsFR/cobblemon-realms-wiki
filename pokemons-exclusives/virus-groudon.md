### 🔮 Item Crafting

![Corrupted Wish crafting recipe](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/LevelsCraft7/cobblemon-realms-wiki/refs/heads/main/imgwiki/Craft-recettes/craft_megashowdown/corrupted_wish.png)

### 🔄 Usage

* 🖱️ Right-click on Groudon with the `Corrupted Wish` item
* ℹ️ No additional conditions are required.

> 💡 The effect can be cancelled by using the item again.

***

### 📜 Description

> Meta Groudon is not a true Groudon, but an artificial creature born from an experiment gone wrong. Corrupted and driven by evil intentions, its sole objective is to absorb the energy of the world around it.
