# 🧭 Getting Started

## 🎒 Your First Steps in Cobblemon Realms

{% hint style="info" %}
<p align="center">
Your adventure in <strong>Cobblemon Realms</strong> begins on <strong>Spawn Island</strong>, the central hub designed to introduce you to the world, its characters, and the main progression systems of the modpack.
</p>
{% endhint %}

---

## 🏝️ 1. Begin on Spawn Island

When you enter a world for the first time, your first objective is simple: **meet Professor Oak and choose your first Pokémon**.

You are free to look around the island, but the intended starting route is clearly guided in-game.

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### 🧭 Find the Spawn Guide

Near the starting area, you can speak with **Mila, the Spawn Guide**.

She will point you toward the most important locations on Spawn Island and can teleport you directly to:

- 🧪 **Professor Oak's Laboratory**
- 🏥 **PokéCenter**
- 🛒 **PokéShop**
- 🏘️ **Village**
- 🏆 **League Hall**

Using Mila is optional, but she is the fastest way to understand the island when starting a new adventure.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### 🧪 Meet Professor Oak

Your first real destination is **Professor Oak's Laboratory**, located on the left side of Spawn Island.

The laboratory can be recognized by its **turning windmill**.

Talk to Professor Oak to begin the starter sequence.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### 🐾 Choose Your Starter Pokémon

Professor Oak will open the starter selection screen and allow you to choose your first Pokémon.

There is no single best choice. Pick the partner you want to begin your journey with.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### 📘 Receive Your Pokédex and Badge Box

After your starter has been selected, Professor Oak will also provide you with:

- 📕 a **Pokédex**, used to follow your Pokémon discoveries;
- 🏅 a **Badge Box**, used to keep track of the Gym Badges earned during your **Gym World Tour**.

These items mark the real beginning of your progression through Cobblemon Realms.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

{% hint style="success" %}
✅ Once you have your starter, Pokédex, and Badge Box, your main adventure is ready to begin.
{% endhint %}

---

## 🗺️ 2. Discover Spawn Island

Spawn Island is more than a starting point. It acts as the main hub for several important systems and contains many NPCs that introduce different parts of the modpack.

| 📍 Location | 🔎 What You Will Find |
| --- | --- |
| 🧪 **Laboratory** | Professor Oak, your starter, and the beginning of your adventure |
| 🏥 **PokéCenter** | A central Pokémon service area and one of the island's main landmarks |
| 🛒 **PokéShop** | Shops and useful supplies for your journey |
| 🏘️ **Village** | Residents, NPCs, and additional points of interest |
| 🏆 **League Hall** | Gym World Tour, League Challenges, Battle Court PvP, and spectating |

{% hint style="info" %}
💡 Take a little time to speak with the NPCs around the island. Several of them introduce mechanics, locations, services, and characters that you may encounter again later.
{% endhint %}

### 🔄 Returning to Spawn Island

You can return to the main hub whenever you need it with:

- `/spawn`
- `/hub`

---

## 🏆 3. Discover the League Hall

The **League Hall** is the main hub for the official Gym progression of Cobblemon Realms.

Talk to the **Arena Host** to learn about the available battle systems.

| ⚔️ Activity | 📖 Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| 🏅 **League Challenge (PvE)** | Battle Gym Leaders and progress through the Gym World Tour |
| 🥊 **Battle Court (PvP)** | Battle other players without affecting your Gym progression |
| 👁️ **Spectator Mode** | Watch League battles already in progress |

Each victory against a Gym Leader awards a **Badge** and unlocks the next eligible step of your Gym World Tour.

You can enter your next League Challenge through the **League Doors** inside the hall. **Arena Entrances** can also be discovered while exploring the Overworld and lead to the same next eligible challenge.

{% hint style="warning" %}
⚠️ The Battle Court is completely separate from the Gym World Tour. PvP battles do not award Gym Badges and do not advance League progression.
{% endhint %}

## 🌍 4. Start Exploring the World

Once your first steps on Spawn Island are complete, you are ready to explore the wider world.

A good early-game routine is to:

- 🪵 Gather your first resources
- ⛏️ Craft tools and useful equipment
- 🏠 Find a place for your first base
- 🐾 Catch new Pokémon and build your team
- 🗺️ Explore biomes, structures, and dimensions
- 📜 Follow quests and progression objectives

Cobblemon Realms contains many custom spawn rules, structures, dimensions, encounters, and progression systems, so exploration is an important part of the experience.

{% hint style="success" %}
📘 Check the [`Pokémon & Spawns`](pokemon-and-spawns.md) page to understand how biome, time, weather, structures, altitude, and other conditions can affect Pokémon encounters.
{% endhint %}

---

## 📖 5. Follow Your Progression

The modpack includes quests, advancements, Gym progression, and other systems designed to guide you over a long-term adventure.

They can help you:

- 🧠 Discover important mechanics
- 🎁 Earn rewards
- 🏅 Track your Gym progress
- 🔓 Unlock new challenges and content
- ⚙️ Understand the modpack's custom systems

{% hint style="info" %}
✨ If you are unsure what to do next, check your quests, advancements, Badge Box, or return to Spawn Island and speak with its NPCs.
{% endhint %}
