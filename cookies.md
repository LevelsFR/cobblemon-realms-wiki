# Cookies & Local Storage

**Last reviewed: 20 November 2026.**

This page explains browser storage and website measurement features visible in the Cobblemon Realms Wiki source.

## What is stored on your device?

The wiki uses browser **localStorage**, including for the light/dark theme, favourite articles, recently visited pages, cached server-status information and your optional-analytics preference. These settings make the requested interface features work and are not advertising identifiers.

Cloudflare's infrastructure may use technical security mechanisms. The exact list of any cookies added at the edge depends on the live hosting configuration and has not been independently verified here. See [Cloudflare's Cookie Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/cookie-policy/).

## Website statistics and search terms

The wiki's own analytics sends events to its first-party backend and, as currently implemented, does not need an analytics cookie. In addition to aggregated events, search terms and optional article feedback can be saved server-side. Details are in the [Website Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy).

The absence of an advertising cookie does not mean that all browsing information is anonymous or that all data processing is exempt from the GDPR.

## Control optional wiki analytics

Use the **Enable/Disable optional analytics** control in the footer of any wiki page. It saves the choice locally and stops subsequent first-party analytics and search telemetry when disabled. Browser Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track are also honoured.

You can clear locally saved preferences via your browser's site-data settings, although doing so also removes your local favourites and other preferences.

A cookie-consent banner is not automatically required merely because functional, consent-exempt storage is used. If non-essential cookies or trackers are introduced, the site must implement the legally required prior consent and withdrawal mechanism before activating them. See [CNIL guidance](https://www.cnil.fr/fr/cookies-et-autres-traceurs/que-dit-la-loi).
