# 📜 Quests & Progression

Cobblemon Realms combines exploration, Pokémon progression and guided activities. This page points to the quest systems documented in the wiki and explains where to start.

## 📱 Rotom Phone Missions (CobbleSafari)

The **Messaging** app on the [Rotom Phone](mods-guides/CobbleSafari/motismart.md) provides quests through conversations with different contacts. A mission can require you to complete an **advancement**, increase a **statistic**, or hand over **items**.

- **Rotom's introductory missions:** the documented CobbleSafari progression unlocks phone apps step by step, generally with one new Rotom mission each day.
- **Other contacts:** some conversations and missions become available only after meeting an advancement or another prerequisite.
- **Rewards:** check the progress bar in the Messaging app and claim a completed mission's reward there.

➡️ [Browse the Rotom Phone mission list](mods-guides/CobbleSafari/list_miss.md).

{% hint style="warning" %}
The mission list documents CobbleSafari's default behavior. **Cobblemon Realms and individual servers can change which missions are enabled, their conditions and their rewards.** Always follow what your current game session displays.
{% endhint %}
